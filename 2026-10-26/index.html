NYC CRE News, Oct 26, 2026 | The Anton Group News of the Day

The 10-year Treasury fell to 5% last week with SOFR at 3.90%, as foreign capital returns to New York office through Tishman Speyer and Bouwinvest's Chrysler Building fund and 350 Park closes a $3.3 billion construction loan. Also: the Kips Bay AMC closes in January as NYU Langone expands, and the City Council passed COPA, giving nonprofits first shot at about 2,100 distressed rental buildings.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:05 · MP3

In this episode

  • The 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 5% last week.
  • SOFR stood at 3.90%.
  • Tishman Speyer and Bouwinvest launched a fund targeting U.S. office, a sign of foreign capital returning to New York office.
  • Vornado, Ken Griffin and Rudin closed the 350 Park Avenue joint venture, anchor lease and $3.3 billion construction loan.
  • AMC's Kips Bay theater is set to close in January.
  • The City Council passed COPA, giving qualified nonprofits a first opportunity to buy certain distressed rental buildings.
