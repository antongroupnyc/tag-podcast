It's Wednesday, October 28th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Tuesday at 4.99%, down 13 basis points from a week ago. SOFR is at 3.93%.

The Fed announces its rate decision at two this afternoon. Futures put the odds of a hold above 90%. Chair Warsh's guidance on a December hike is what moves your refinancing math.

Shorenstein is buying a fully leased SoHo office building on Lafayette Street for $132.5 million. Tishman Speyer bought it just last year, and well-leased downtown office is drawing institutional bids again.

And Japan's Takenaka is under contract to buy One Broadway for $170 million. Foreign capital is still paying up for fully leased Manhattan landmarks.

One more. A three-building Kips Bay walkup complex traded for $12 million, less than half its 2015 price. Older walkups are repricing hard, opening the door for patient capital.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
