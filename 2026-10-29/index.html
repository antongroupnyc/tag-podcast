71 Fifth Leasehold Trades for $62M, St. Brigid Site $35M, Fed Holds | NYC CRE News Oct 29, 2026

It's Thursday, October 29th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton. The 10-year Treasury closed Wednesday at 5.05%. SOFR is at 3.95%. The Fed held rates at 3.75% to 4% yesterday. Two members voted for a hike, so keep that risk in your refinancing math. Namdar bought the leasehold at 71 Fifth Avenue in the Flatiron for $62 million. That's far below its 2021 pricing talk, and Manhattan office is still finding its true level. The Archdiocese is selling the former St. Brigid School site on Tompkins Square Park for $35 million. Quinlan plans an 11-story rental there, as church land keeps feeding new housing. One more. Savanna and Deutsche Bank are pushing off the foreclosure sale of 521 Fifth Avenue again. A June appraisal valued the tower at $270 million, well below what Savanna paid. That's New York. See you tomorrow.
