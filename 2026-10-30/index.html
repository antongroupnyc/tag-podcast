Weil Weighs Wind-Down at GM Building, BXP Talks 7 Times Square Sale at $750M | NYC CRE News Oct 30, 2026

It's Friday, October 30th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton. The 10-year Treasury closed Thursday at 4.98%. SOFR is at 3.94%. Weil Gotshal's leaders have recommended winding down the firm, with a partner vote expected in the coming days. Weil fills about 400,000 square feet at the GM Building, so a big block of trophy space on Fifth Avenue could come back to market. BXP posted third-quarter revenue of $911 million, up 4.6%. It's also negotiating to sell 7 Times Square for up to $750 million. Big REITs are selling Midtown towers to fund new development, and that keeps large office trades coming. BXP is also in talks with a tenant for five floors at 343 Madison. That would take the new tower to nearly 70% pre-leased, more proof that new trophy space still wins. One more. On this day in 1938, Orson Welles broadcast The War of the Worlds live from the Columbia Broadcasting Building at 485 Madison Avenue. It was staged as breaking news about Martians landing in New Jersey. An estimated 6 million people tuned in, and Welles was just 23. That's New York. Have a great weekend.
