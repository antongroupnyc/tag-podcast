Shorenstein Buys 148 Lafayette for $132.5M | NYC CRE News Nov 2, 2026

Fannie and Freddie tighten lending after the rent freeze, Shorenstein buys 148 Lafayette for $132.5M, Gentle Monster takes 28,500 SF at the Puck Building, and Eataly Caffe opens at 2252 Broadway.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:01 · MP3

Transcript

It's Monday, November 2nd. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Friday at 4.94%. SOFR is at 3.94%.

After the rent freeze, Fannie Mae now assumes no rent increases on stabilized units for four to eight years, and Freddie Mac has paused new loans. For owners of fully regulated buildings, refinancing just got much harder.

Shorenstein bought 148 Lafayette Street in SoHo for $132.5 million. The building is fully leased, proof that well-leased office still draws big buyers.

Gentle Monster is taking 28,500 square feet at the Puck Building in SoHo. Kushner Companies says that completes the building's re-tenanting, another sign SoHo flagship retail is filling up.

Eataly Caffe opens Friday at 2252 Broadway, in the Upper West Side Starbucks space made famous by You've Got Mail. A famous corner with a fresh tenant keeps Broadway retail moving.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
