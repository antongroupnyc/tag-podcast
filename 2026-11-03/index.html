Transcript

It's Tuesday, November 3rd. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

It's Election Day, so get out and vote. Polls are open 6 a.m. to 9 p.m. City voters also decide five ballot questions, including one to simplify the construction permitting process, which matters to everyone who builds in New York.

The 10-year Treasury closed Monday at 4.91%. SOFR is at 3.95%.

Northwind Group is lending $231 million to turn 675 Third Avenue, near Grand Central, into as many as 450 apartments. A quarter will be affordable, and office-to-resi conversions keep getting funded.

Brightshore Capital bought DŌMI, a new 190-unit rental complex in Long Island City, for just over $143 million. New Queens multifamily is still drawing big buyers.

Manhattan office leasing is on pace for its best year since 2000, projected at nearly 44 million square feet.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
