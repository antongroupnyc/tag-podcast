35 W 57th St $42M, Kushner $12M Sale | NYC CRE News Nov 4, 2026

Voters pass Question 4 on construction permitting, Freddie Mac reportedly pauses rent-regulated loans, Sedesco buys 35 W 57th St for $42M, and Kushner sells three Murray Hill walk-ups for $12M.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:02 · MP3

Transcript

It's Wednesday, November 4th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Tuesday at 4.95%. SOFR is at 3.95%.

City voters approved Question 4 with about 67% of the vote. It creates one central system for construction permits across city agencies. Faster approvals mean lower carrying costs for anyone who builds here.

Freddie Mac has reportedly paused new loans on the city's rent-regulated buildings while it weighs the rent freeze, and Fannie Mae has tightened its underwriting. For stabilized owners, agency refinancing just got harder.

Sedesco paid $42 million for 35 West 57th Street, a Gilded Age mansion on Billionaires' Row. It fills in the developer's supertall assemblage, a reminder that patient site assembly still drives Midtown development.

Kushner Companies sold three Murray Hill walk-ups, including rent-stabilized units, for $12 million, less than half what it paid in 2015. Mixed-regulation buildings are trading at a deep reset.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
