BofA, Citi Manhattan Retail, Vornado 25.9% | NYC CRE News Nov 5, 2026

Banks grab Manhattan retail as availability hits a record low, Vornado's 25.9% office mark-to-market, record Manhattan rents, and a single DOB permitting hub.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:01 · MP3

Transcript

It's Thursday, November 5th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Wednesday at 4.90%. SOFR is at 3.97%.

Bank of America and Citibank took two of Manhattan's biggest third-quarter retail leases as prime-corridor availability fell to a record-low 157 spaces. Banks are back fighting for storefronts, a clear read that avenue retail demand is strong again.

Vornado's Manhattan office re-leasing posted cash rents up 25.9% in the third quarter. That mark-to-market supports pricing for well-leased Class A towers across the city.

Manhattan's median asking rent hit a record $5,375 a month last quarter. Tight free-market inventory keeps pushing rents higher for owners and investors alike.

City charter changes put about 40 construction approvals under one Buildings Department hub. Faster permitting cuts carry time for renovations and conversions across New York City.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
