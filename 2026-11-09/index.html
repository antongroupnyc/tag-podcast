210 NYC Opportunity Zones (96 Fewer), 60 Wall $400M | NYC CRE News Nov 9, 2026

Treasury designated 210 Opportunity Zone tracts in NYC, 96 fewer than the prior 306; Bushburg is under contract for 60 Wall Street at $400 million; Atlas Capital buys 360 West 43rd for $142.5 million; and the Heisman Trophy leases at 120 Broadway.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:04 · MP3

Transcript

It's Monday, November 9th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Friday at 4.86%. SOFR is at 3.92%.

Treasury designated 210 Opportunity Zone tracts in New York City, 96 fewer than the prior 306. The permanent program resets every 10 years with a 5-year gain deferral, and the map takes effect January 1.

Bushburg Properties is under contract to buy 60 Wall Street from GIC for $400 million, less than half its 2017 price. Conversions are Bushburg's specialty, so watch whether another vacant Financial District tower goes residential.

Atlas Capital Group bought 360 West 43rd Street in Hell's Kitchen off-market for $142.5 million. Investors are still paying up for well-located Manhattan rental buildings.

The Heisman Trophy is moving downtown. Heisman signed a 10-year lease at Silverstein's landmarked 120 Broadway. Older Lower Manhattan towers with strong amenities keep winning tenants.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
