501 Seventh Ave $150M, 484 Broadway $33.5M | NYC CRE News Nov 10, 2026

ESRT is marketing 501 Seventh Avenue for about $150 million, Lagfin sold 484-486 Broadway for $33.5 million, Northwind provided a $231 million loan at 675 Third Avenue, and the city plans a mid-2027 tax lien sale.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:06 · MP3

Transcript

It's Tuesday, November 10th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Monday at 4.88%. SOFR is at 3.89%.

City Hall says it will hold one more property tax lien sale in mid-2027, reversing the mayor's campaign pledge to end it. Owners carrying unpaid tax or water debt should clear it before the city's notice list comes out early next year.

Empire State Realty Trust is marketing 501 Seventh Avenue in the Garment District for about $150 million. A well-leased fashion tenant building will show what buyers pay for Midtown office today.

Lagfin, the family office behind Campari, sold 484 to 486 Broadway in SoHo for $33.5 million. The buyer is reportedly linked to a private Asian investor, more proof that foreign money still wants SoHo.

Northwind Group provided a $231 million construction loan to turn 675 Third Avenue from office into rentals. Lenders are still funding Midtown office-to-residential conversions.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
