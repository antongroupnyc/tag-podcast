COPA Signed, 110 E 16th $44.25M | NYC CRE News Nov 11, 2026

Transcript

It's Wednesday, November 11th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

Today is Veterans Day, and we thank every veteran who served. It began as Armistice Day, when World War One fighting stopped at 11 a.m. on November 11, 1918.

The 10-year Treasury closed Tuesday at 4.98%. SOFR is at 3.88%.

Mayor Mamdani signed the Community Opportunity to Purchase Act on Tuesday. It gives qualified nonprofits first chance to buy about 2,100 covered buildings. Owners selling those multifamily buildings should plan for a longer sale process starting next November.

Oppenheimer signed a lease for about 220,000 square feet at RXR's 32 Old Slip, moving its headquarters from 85 Broad Street. A major financial firm staying downtown is a vote of confidence in Lower Manhattan office.

Tishman sold the 110 East 16th Street development site near Union Square for $44.25 million. The buyer plans a condo tower, a bet that new Manhattan condo supply stays tight.

Extell paid $18 million for 1190 Avenue of the Americas, adding land to its Ikea-anchored tower at 574 Fifth Avenue. Assemblages are still how big Midtown sites get built.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
