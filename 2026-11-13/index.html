Extell $34.9M Air Rights, Metis $52M | NYC CRE News Nov 13, 2026

Extell's $34.9M air rights buy for 655 Madison, CSC in contract for the Episcopal Church Center, Metis pays $52M for 100 Lenox Road, and the Holland Tunnel.

Transcript

It's Friday, November 13th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Thursday at 5.10%. SOFR is at 3.89%.

Extell paid $34.9 million to Hartz Mountain for the air rights over 34 and 36 East 61st Street. They add development potential to its planned supertall condo tower at 655 Madison. Air rights remain one of the fastest ways to grow a Manhattan site.

CSC Real Estate is under contract to buy the Episcopal Church Center at 815 Second Avenue, a largely vacant office building at East 43rd Street. Terms were not disclosed. CSC specializes in office-to-residential conversions, and empty Midtown East offices are prime candidates.

Metis Equities paid $52 million for 100 Lenox Road, a redeveloped rental building in Brooklyn's Prospect Lefferts Gardens. Investors are still paying up for newer Brooklyn rentals near Prospect Park.

Dates Matter. November 13, 1927: the Holland Tunnel opened to traffic. Its Manhattan mouth is now Hudson Square, where Anthropic leased all 466,000 square feet of 330 Hudson Street this year. AI tenants are now anchoring the neighborhood's office demand.

That's New York. Have a great weekend.
