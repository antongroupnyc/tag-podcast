Transcript

It's Monday, November 16th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Friday at 5.15%. SOFR is at 3.90%.

The city is now inspecting 160 construction sites tied to firms on the project next to the deadly collapse at 1321 Flatbush Avenue. Owners should expect tougher scrutiny of excavation and permits citywide.

The City Council approved Roundabout Theatre's Studio 54 plan, creating about 188,000 square feet of bonus floor area in exchange for a theater rehab. That adds new Midtown air rights to the market.

Manhattan buyers signed contracts on 45 homes asking $4 million or more last week, the highest weekly volume since 2021.

Dolce and Gabbana leased the full 9th floor at 551 Fifth Avenue for its New York headquarters. Fifth Avenue office space is still drawing luxury brands.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
