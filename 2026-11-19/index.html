Rockrose $309M refi, EMP Capital at 1119 Pacific | NYC CRE News Nov 19, 2026

Rockrose refinances 11 East 26th Street for $309 million, the MTA approves EMP Capital for 375 apartments at 1119 Pacific Street, the state housing agency clarifies rent concessions, and the Department of Buildings issues stop-work orders tied to last week's Flatbush collapse.

Transcript

It's Thursday, November 19th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Wednesday at 5.07%. SOFR is at 3.92%.

Rockrose refinanced its boutique office property at 11 East 26th Street with a $309 million, 10-year loan from Goldman Sachs. The two-building complex sits at the north end of Madison Square Park.

The MTA board approved EMP Capital on Wednesday to build 375 apartments at 1119 Pacific Street in Crown Heights, including about 90 affordable units. The project will provide $47 million to the authority.

The state's housing agency updated its guidance this month, clarifying that rent concessions can be distinct from preferential rents after a wave of lawsuits.

The Department of Buildings issued stop-work orders on at least five projects tied to the engineers on last week's Flatbush collapse, and has inspected at least 24 job sites across four boroughs.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
