A Takenaka Development affiliate buys 1 Broadway in the Financial District for $165 million, prime Midtown office availability falls to 1.9%, and Rockrose closes a $309 million Goldman Sachs refinancing on 11 East 26th Street, a building the Anton Group assembled from more than 57 tenants in common.

Transcript

It's Monday, November 23rd. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Friday at 4.92%. SOFR is at 3.91%.

An affiliate of Takenaka Development bought One Broadway in the Financial District for $165 million, Friday's largest commercial deal.

Availability in Midtown's prime office buildings fell to 1.9% this year, pushing rents higher.

Last week Rockrose closed a $309 million, 10-year Goldman Sachs refinancing on 11 East 26th Street, on the north end of Madison Square Park. That building was once held by more than 57 owners as tenants in common, with no one able to sell it. Over four and a half years, much of it during the COVID lockdowns, our team at the Anton Group closed 15 separate transactions with those owners, leading to a partition auction that delivered the entire asset to Rockrose at a $275 million valuation.

That's New York. See you tomorrow.
