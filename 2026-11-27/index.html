Rent Freeze Upheld, 530 Fifth $122M Loan | NYC CRE News Nov 27, 2026

Judge upholds NYC rent freeze, $122.1M refinancing at 530 Fifth Avenue, top NYC lenders $36B, and the first Macys parade.

Eric Michael Anton · 1:13 · MP3

Transcript

It's Friday, November 27th. Welcome to the Anton Group News of the Day, I'm Eric Michael Anton.

The 10-year Treasury closed Wednesday at 4.85%. SOFR is at 3.94%.

A Manhattan judge on Wednesday upheld the Rent Guidelines Board's two-year rent freeze for roughly 1 million stabilized apartments, rejecting a landlord challenge. The owners' lawyer says they are considering next steps, including a possible appeal.

Aurora Capital Associates and AVRS Partners refinanced their retail condominium at 530 Fifth Avenue with a one hundred twenty-two point 1 million dollar loan from Rialto Capital Management. The three-level space near Grand Central is fully leased, with tenants including Chase Bank and Walgreens.

The city's twenty biggest real estate lenders made $36 billion in loans in the twelve months through July, based on recorded mortgages. Wells Fargo led the ranking, a sign that bank credit for New York deals is flowing again.

Dates Matter. November twenty-seventh, nineteen twenty-four: Macy's first parade ended at Herald Square, marking the opening of its expanded store along Thirty-fourth Street. This year the hundredth parade was postponed by weather for the first time ever, and steps off Saturday at eight thirty.

That's New York. Have a great weekend.
